Election Campaign

Rules, spending limits, and MCC

Model Code of Conduct (MCC)

  • Comes into force immediately on election announcement
  • Applies to all political parties, candidates, Union & State governments
  • Remains in effect until results are declared

What MCC Prohibits

  • Using government resources (vehicles, bungalows) for campaigning
  • Announcing new welfare schemes after MCC kicks in
  • Hate speech, communal appeals, caste-based campaigning
  • Bribing voters with cash, liquor, or gifts
  • Campaigning within 100 metres of polling booth (Silent Zone)
  • Campaigning in 48-hour Silent Period before polling (Section 126, RPA 1951)

Campaigning Methods Allowed

  • Public rallies & road shows (with prior police permission)
  • Door-to-door canvassing (max 5 people including candidate)
  • Print, TV & digital advertisements
  • Social media campaigns (must be pre-certified by Media Certification & Monitoring Committee)

Expenditure Rules

  • Candidate must maintain a daily account of all expenses
  • Submit complete expenditure statement within 30 days of result declaration
  • ECI deploys Flying Squads and Expenditure Observers to monitor violations
  • Exceeding limit = disqualification under Section 123(6), RPA 1951

Paid Political Advertisements

  • All TV/radio/digital ads must be pre-certified by MCMC (Media Certification & Monitoring Committee)
  • Surrogate advertising (disguised brand ads) is prohibited
  • Exit polls cannot be published until last phase of polling ends
