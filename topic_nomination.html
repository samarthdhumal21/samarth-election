Nomination Process

How candidates file to run for office

Step-by-Step Process

Step 1 — Gazette Notification
ECI publishes Official Gazette Notification setting the last date for nominations.
Step 2 — Filing Nomination (Form 2A)
Candidate submits nomination papers to the Returning Officer (RO) along with:
  • Form 26 — Affidavit declaring criminal record, assets & liabilities, educational qualifications
  • Proposers' signatures (1 for general seats, 10 for reserved seats)
  • Security deposit — Rs. 25,000 (Lok Sabha), Rs. 12,500 (Assembly)
  • SC/ST candidates pay 50% of security deposit
Step 3 — Scrutiny
RO examines all nomination papers. Grounds for rejection:
  • Candidate not qualified
  • Proposer not registered voter of constituency
  • Incomplete or fraudulent affidavit
Step 4 — Withdrawal
Candidates may withdraw within a statutory period (usually 2 days after scrutiny) before 3:00 PM.
Step 5 — Final Candidate List
RO publishes the final list of contesting candidates in the Official Gazette.

Key Rules

  • Maximum 4 nomination papers per candidate
  • Candidate can contest from maximum 2 constituencies simultaneously
  • Security deposit forfeited if candidate gets less than 1/6th of valid votes polled

Disqualification from Contesting

  • Convicted and sentenced to 2+ years imprisonment
  • Dismissed from government service for corruption
  • Director/Managing Agent of a government company
  • Holding an office of profit under the government
