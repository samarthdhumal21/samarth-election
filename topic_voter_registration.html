Voter Registration

Learn how to get on the electoral roll

Who Can Register?

  • Indian citizen, 18+ years of age as on January 1 of the election year
  • Ordinarily resident of the constituency
  • Not disqualified under any law (conviction, unsound mind, non-citizenship)

How to Register?

  • Online: voters.eci.gov.in → Fill Form 6
  • Offline: Submit Form 6 at Electoral Registration Officer (ERO) office
  • Via BLO: Your Booth Level Officer visits homes for registration

Key Forms

Form Purpose
Form 6New voter registration
Form 6AOverseas Indian voters
Form 7Objection to a name
Form 8Correction of details
Form 8ATransfer to another constituency

Important Facts

  • Electoral Roll is updated every year before January 1
  • EPIC (Electors Photo Identity Card) is issued after registration
  • Alternative IDs accepted: Aadhaar, PAN, Passport, Driving Licence
  • NRI voters can register under Section 20A of RPA, 1950
  • Special Summary Revision happens annually — check your name every year
  • Minimum age to vote: 18 years (lowered from 21 by 61st Constitutional Amendment, 1988)

Disqualifications

  • Non-citizen of India
  • Convicted and imprisoned
  • Of unsound mind (declared by competent court)
  • Corrupt practice under RPA 1951
