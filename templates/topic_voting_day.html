Voting Day

EVM, VVPAT, and booth process

Before Polling Begins

  • Mock Poll conducted in presence of party agents
  • EVM tested with sample votes — result verified and cleared
  • Presiding Officer seals Ballot Unit compartment
  • Polling agents of all candidates take positions

Voting Hours

7:00 AM to 6:00 PM (standard)
Extended hours in certain states/constituencies. Anyone in queue at closing time must be allowed to vote.

Step-by-Step Booth Process

  1. Step 1 → Voter enters booth, presents ID to Polling Officer
  2. Step 2 → Polling Officer verifies name in Electoral Roll, marks register
  3. Step 3 → Indelible ink applied to left index finger (prevents double voting)
  4. Step 4 → Voter signs/thumbprints Form 17A (Register of Voters)
  5. Step 5 → Presiding Officer presses Ballot Button on Control Unit
  6. Step 6 → Voter enters screened compartment, presses button on Ballot Unit
  7. Step 7 → VVPAT prints slip — visible for 7 seconds through glass window
  8. Step 8 → Slip drops into sealed VVPAT drop-box. Beep confirms vote recorded
  9. Step 9 → Voter exits booth

EVM Key Facts

  • Max 2,000 votes per EVM
  • Max 384 candidates (24 Ballot Units)
  • 6V alkaline battery — no electricity needed
  • No internet, Bluetooth, or wireless connection
  • Tamper-proof — standalone machine

VVPAT Facts

  • Mandated by Supreme Court in Subramanian Swamy v. ECI (2013)
  • Paper slip visible for 7 seconds
  • 2% of VVPATs verified before result certification
  • Accessible only to authorised polling officials

Indelible Ink

  • Applied on left index finger
  • Manufactured by Mysore Paints & Varnish Ltd
  • Stays for 2–4 weeks — impossible to remove
  • Purpose: Prevent double voting
