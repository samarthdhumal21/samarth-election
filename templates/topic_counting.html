Counting & Results

How votes are tallied and declared

Before Counting

  • EVMs stored in Strong Rooms under double-lock system
  • 24×7 CCTV surveillance + two-tier security (CAPF)
  • RO visits strong rooms twice daily
  • Candidates/agents can observe strong rooms

Counting Day Process

Step 1 — Postal Ballots First
Postal ballots are counted before EVM votes. Results announced separately.
Step 2 — EVM Counting Rounds
Each round = votes from one polling booth's EVM. Results displayed on Control Unit screen. Agents of all candidates observe each round. Results recorded in Form 20 (Final Result Sheet).
Step 3 — VVPAT Verification
2% of VVPATs per constituency randomly selected. Physical paper slips matched against EVM count. Any mismatch triggers investigation.
Step 4 — Round-by-Round Tabulation
All booth results added up. Running totals updated after each round. Trends visible from Round 1 itself.
Step 5 — Declaration of Result
Returning Officer announces the winner. Winner receives Certificate of Election. Results notified in the Official Gazette.

Key Rules

  • Counting starts at 8:00 AM on Counting Day
  • Conducted under Election Observer supervision
  • Strict entry — only authorised persons with valid passes
  • Mobile phones not allowed inside counting hall
  • Re-counting can be demanded if margin is very thin

Re-Poll Conditions

ECI can order re-polling if:

  • Booth capturing occurred
  • EVM malfunctioned seriously
  • Natural disaster disrupted polling
  • Post-poll scrutiny reveals serious irregularities

Election Petition

  • Filed in High Court within 45 days of result declaration
  • Grounds: Bribery, corruption, impersonation, exceeding expenditure limit
  • Court can declare election void or declare another candidate winner
