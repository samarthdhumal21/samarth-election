Election Commission

The body that runs it all

Constitutional Foundation

  • Established: 25 January 1950 (National Voters' Day)
  • Constitutional Provision: Article 324
  • Nature: Independent, autonomous constitutional body

Composition

  • 1 Chief Election Commissioner (CEC)
  • 2 Election Commissioners
  • Total: 3-member body

Key Powers (Article 324)

Article Power
324(1)Superintendence & control of all elections
324(2)Composition of ECI determined by President
324(5)CEC removable only by impeachment
327Parliament frames election laws

Functions of ECI

  • Announces election schedule & enforces MCC
  • Recognises & deregisters political parties
  • Allots election symbols to parties & candidates
  • Deploys observers, flying squads & CAPF
  • Oversees voter registration & electoral rolls
  • Certifies electronic media political advertisements
  • Settles disputes on party mergers & defections
  • Conducts delimitation in consultation with Delimitation Commission

Key Officials ECI Deploys

Official Role
Chief Electoral Officer (CEO)State-level ECI representative
District Election Officer (DEO)District-level logistics
Returning Officer (RO)Constituency-level conduct
Election ObserverIndependent monitoring
Micro ObserverBooth-level monitoring
BLOVoter list maintenance

Protection of CEC

  • Cannot be removed except by impeachment — same as Supreme Court Judge
  • Tenure: 6 years or age 65, whichever is earlier
  • Salary charged to Consolidated Fund of India (not subject to Parliament vote)
  • Ensures complete independence from government
