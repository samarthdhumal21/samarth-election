Frequently Asked Questions

How do I register to vote?

Visit voters.eci.gov.in and fill out Form 6. You need to be 18+ and have a valid address proof.

What if I forgot my Voter ID?

You can use one of 12 alternative IDs like Aadhaar, PAN Card, Driving License, or Passport to cast your vote.

How can I find my polling booth?

You can search using your EPIC number on the ECI website or the Voter Helpline App.
